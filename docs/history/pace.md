## Pace and stop rules

The process targets a runnable result within an increment's first hour and a
reviewed increment within roughly two hours. Stages contain several increments;
those targets are not estimates for an entire stage. Record an estimate before
each increment and reassess stalls after 30 minutes. Complete and verify the
interaction before expanding it. Do not trade away acceptance to meet a time box.

Use S0/S1's observed progress to forecast the next stages. No reliable calendar
completion date exists yet; measure implementation and verification time before
promising one. Solver integration and constraint-preserving curve edits are the
main known uncertainties; each gets a bounded, product-specific investigation.
The existing rectangle proof is not evidence that either is already solved.

Pause at a usable review or a real unresolved product choice. Do not stop merely
because an internal module is done, and do not cross a product review into the
next tool automatically. The current delivery position above determines where to
resume; do not re-request roadmap approval at the start of each increment.
