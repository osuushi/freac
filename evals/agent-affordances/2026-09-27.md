## Interpretation and next question

The useful observation is reduced discovery in one filtered-selection example.
This is one sample per condition with fixed ordering, shared workspace and differing
cache hits: the times do not establish a repeatable speedup or general model quality.
Both conditions already had the new operating guide, so baseline success on the
simple command does not disprove the founder's experience with the former full setup.

The next question is how to make query → predicate → one final selection the natural
path, without adding another warning tied specifically to cylinders. A later experiment
can compare an explicit general read/compute/apply pattern or a more direct query/action
entry point. The current results do not justify adding another special-case CLI flag.
No additional model runs or production prompt changes were made after this comparison.
